# How to delete your King Of Anime account

You can delete your King Of Anime account in two ways:

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open King Of Anime.
2. Tap the profile icon in the top-right of the home screen.
3. Tap **Settings**.
4. Tap **"Delete my account"**.
5. Confirm twice.

Your account and all associated data are permanently deleted within 24 hours.

## By email

Send an email to **kingofanimeofficial1@gmail.com** with the subject "Delete my account" and include your username. We will delete your account within 30 days.

---

## What gets deleted

- Your username and avatar
- Your level, XP, and game-progression data
- Your coins and shurikens balance
- Your unlocked series
- Your leaderboard history
- Your daily mission progress

## What we retain

- Anonymised aggregate statistics (not linked to you)
- Logs of the deletion request, for up to 2 years (legal compliance)
