# King Of Anime — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-05-02
**Last updated:** 2026-05-02

This Privacy Policy describes how King Of Anime ("**King Of Anime**", "**we**", "**us**", "**our**") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use the King Of Anime mobile application (the "**App**") and any related websites or services (together, the "**Services**").

We designed King Of Anime to collect as little personal data as possible. This policy is written to be understandable; capitalised terms have the meanings given in our [Terms of Service](/terms).

## 2. Data we collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide the Services.

### 2.1 Data you provide directly

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Account profile | Username (chosen by you), selected avatar | Display inside the App and on the leaderboard |
| Gameplay data | Scores, streaks, XP, coin balance, completed missions, game-session history | Deliver gameplay, progression, leaderboards |
| Support requests | The contents of any email or in-app feedback you send us | Respond to you |

We do **not** collect your real name, email address (unless you email us), phone number, date of birth (beyond a self-declared "I am 13+" age gate), home address, payment details, biometric data, or precise geolocation.

### 2.2 Data collected automatically

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Device / technical data | Device model, OS version, app version, language, approximate region (country-level only) | Diagnostics, regional content |
| Advertising identifiers | Apple IDFA (iOS), Google Advertising ID (Android) — **only** if you grant consent | Show personalised or non-personalised ads |
| IP address | Transiently, during network requests | Security, rate limiting, coarse geo-routing |

### 2.3 Data we do NOT collect

- Real name, birthdate, email (except where you voluntarily send us one), phone number
- Precise location (GPS)
- Contacts, photos, microphone, camera
- Payment / credit-card data

## 7. Security

We implement TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit, AES-256 at rest (via Supabase), row-level security policies, and regular dependency patching.

## 8. Children & minors

King Of Anime is rated **13+** (App Store) / **Teen** (Google Play). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data to us, contact kingofanimeofficial1@gmail.com and we will delete it within 10 business days.
