## 2. Data we collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide the Services. The following is an exhaustive list:

### 2.1 Data you provide directly

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Account profile | Username (chosen by you), selected avatar | Display inside the App and on the leaderboard |
| Gameplay data | Scores, streaks, XP, coin balance, completed missions, game-session history | Deliver gameplay, progression, leaderboards |
| Support requests | The contents of any email or in-app feedback you send us | Respond to you |

We do **not** collect your real name, email address (unless you email us), phone number, date of birth (beyond a self-declared "I am 13+ / 16+" age gate), home address, payment details, biometric data, or precise geolocation.

### 2.2 Data collected automatically

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Device / technical data | Device model, OS version, app version, language, approximate region (derived from IP country-level only) | Diagnostics, crash reporting, regional content |
| Advertising identifiers | Apple IDFA (iOS), Google Advertising ID (Android) — **only** if you grant consent via the ATT prompt (iOS) or the UMP consent (EU/UK/EEA) | Show non-personalised or (with consent) personalised ads |
| Diagnostic / crash data | Stack traces, anonymous session identifiers | Fix bugs, improve stability |
| IP address | Transiently, during network requests | Security, rate limiting, coarse geo-routing |

We do **not** use cookies on the App itself (mobile native) but our website (if any) may use strictly-necessary cookies only.

### 2.3 Data we do NOT collect

- Real name, birthdate, email (except where you voluntarily send us one), phone number
- Precise location (GPS)
- Contacts, photos, microphone, camera
- Social-network identities (no Facebook/Google sign-in in v1.0)
- Payment / credit-card data — the App does not sell items directly in v1.0

## 7. Security

We implement commercially reasonable technical and organisational measures, including:

- Transport encryption (HTTPS / TLS 1.2+) for all network traffic
- At-rest encryption in Supabase (managed by the provider)
- Row-level security policies limiting each user to their own data
- Principle-of-least-privilege access for engineers
- Secret scanning in source control
- Regular dependency patching

No security measure is perfect. In the unlikely event of a personal-data breach likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours and, where required, notify affected users without undue delay.
